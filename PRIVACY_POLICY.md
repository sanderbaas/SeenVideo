# Privacy Policy for SeenVideo

**Last Updated: September 2026**

SeenVideo ("we", "us", or "our") operates the SeenVideo mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our service to you.

### Types of Data Collected
*   **Google Account Information:** When you sign in via Google, we access your email address, name, and profile picture to personalize your experience.
*   **YouTube Data:** The App requests access to your YouTube account to:
    *   Retrieve your "Watch Later" playlist.
    *   Manage a dedicated "SeenVideo Watched" playlist to track videos you have marked as seen.
*   **Local Data:** We store your followed channels and video status (seen/unseen) in a local database on your device.

## 2. Use of Data
We use the collected data to:
*   Synchronize your video watch status across devices via YouTube playlists.
*   Provide a filtered view of your YouTube subscriptions.
*   Maintain your session so you don't have to log in repeatedly.

## 3. Data Storage and Security
*   **No Third-Party Servers:** We do **not** store your personal data or YouTube information on our own servers. All data is stored either locally on your device or within your own Google/YouTube account.
*   **Security:** We use standard Android security measures and the official Google Identity Services to protect your authentication tokens.

## 4. Disclosure of Data
We do **not** sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personal data to third parties. Your data is only used to facilitate the interaction between the App and YouTube.

## 5. Your Data Protection Rights
You can:
*   **Disconnect:** Sign out of the App at any time to stop data synchronization.
*   **Delete Data:** Clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App will remove all locally stored data.
*   **Revoke Access:** You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time via your [Google Security Settings](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

## 6. Contact Us
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the project's repository.
