SeenVideo gives you a focused way to follow YouTube channels and keep track of the videos you want to watch—without signing in or adding another feed full of distractions.

Add channels by searching or entering a handle, such as @VPROTegenlicht. Your home feed shows new videos only from channels you choose, and custom groups help you organize them your way.

Make your feed your own
- Follow specific YouTube channels by handle or with built-in search.
- Organize channels into custom groups such as News or Entertainment.
- Filter your home feed instantly with interactive group pills.
- Filter out YouTube Shorts for a more long-form viewing experience.

Keep track of videos locally
- Add videos to your local Watch Later queue.
- Mark videos as watched and manage their status on your device.
- Share videos from the YouTube app to SeenVideo to queue them for later or mark them as watched.

Stay focused
- Blur titles and thumbnails for selected channels; tap to reveal them.
- Highlight or filter keywords in video titles.
- Choose a daily notification time for one bundled summary of new videos.
- Turn notifications on or off for individual channels.

SeenVideo features a clean Material 3 interface with Dark Mode support. It’s local-first: no account login or cloud sync is required, and your data stays on your device.
